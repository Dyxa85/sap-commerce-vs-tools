# Types and diagrams

- **Go to Type, Attribute or Enum Value…** (`Cmd+Alt+T` / `Ctrl+Alt+T`) searches all loaded extensions.
- **Show Type** previews inheritance, attributes, subtypes and relations.
- **Type Diagram**, **Extension Dependency Diagram** and **Business Process Diagram** draw graphs you can pan and zoom;
  click a node to open it.
- `Cmd/Ctrl+click` on a type, attribute, bean id or `class="…"` in XML or ImpEx jumps to the definition.
